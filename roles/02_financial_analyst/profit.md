## Handoff

### Financial analyst recommendation: do not invest at present

I recommend **passing on the current $500,000 investment request**. NovaCart has demonstrated revenue growth, but the reconstructed income statements show worsening losses and material differences from management’s presentation. Revenue after refunds increased from **$300,000 in 2024 to $520,000 in 2025**, or **73.3%**. Operating costs rose faster, from **$330,000 to $610,000**, or **84.8%**. Consequently, the annual loss increased from **$30,000 to $90,000**, and the operating margin deteriorated from **−10.0% to −17.3%**. The present evidence does not demonstrate that expansion funding would produce profitable growth.[^claims][^bank24][^bank25]

### Duplicate records requiring correction

**Three invoices appear twice in the 2025 register, overstating the raw invoice total by $325,000.** Each invoice has only one corresponding bank receipt, so its second register entry must be excluded.[^duplicates]

| Invoice ID | Customer | Amount of duplicate copy to remove | Invoice file lines | Bank receipt line |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- | ---: |
| NC-2025-0074 | North Coast Workplace Meals | $100,000 | 690–691 | 750 |
| NC-2025-0327 | Summit Hospitality Group | $110,000 | 885–886 | 959 |
| NC-2025-0801 | Metro Campus Dining | $115,000 | 1341–1342 | 1439 |
| **Total** | | **$325,000** | | |

Invoice lines refer to `inputs/invoices.csv`; receipt lines refer to `inputs/bank.csv`.[^duplicates] These duplicates explain part of the discrepancy, but removing them alone does not produce cash-basis revenue. The complete reconciliation is **$1,000,000 raw invoices − $325,000 duplicates − $85,000 unpaid invoices − $40,000 collected in 2026 − $30,000 refunds = $520,000 revenue**. Management’s $1,200,000 claim therefore exceeds supported 2025 revenue by **$680,000**. The records establish reporting errors and collection/timing differences; they do not establish deliberate fraud.[^inv25][^open][^late][^claims][^bank25]

### Pros: reasons to reconsider an investment later

- **Meaningful verified growth:** net revenue increased by $220,000, or 73.3%, even after correcting the register and applying cash-basis accounting. This is a positive indication of increased collected business.[^bank24][^bank25]
- **Some demonstrated quarterly profitability:** Q1 and Q2 2025 generated operating profits of $28,917 and $8,765 respectively. These periods show that collections exceeded paid operating costs in parts of the year, although they do not establish recurring profitability.[^bank25]
- **A verifiable financial baseline:** the 2024 revenue claim agrees with bank-supported net revenue, and the supplied bank records permit reconciliation of the 2025 discrepancies. Corrected reporting would give the team a stronger basis for evaluating a future proposal.[^claims][^bank24][^bank25][^inv25]

### Cons: reasons to decline the current request

- **Growth is increasing losses:** 2025 operating costs consumed 117.3% of net revenue. The $60,000 deterioration in annual profit indicates that revenue growth has not translated into sustainable earnings.[^bank24][^bank25]
- **Recent performance is weak:** Q3 lost $1,387 and Q4 lost $126,295. Q4 net revenue was only $48,705 against $175,000 of operating costs. This contradicts management’s claim of profitability since Q3 and makes the latest operating trend a central concern.[^claims][^bank25]
- **Reporting reliability needs improvement:** duplicate entries inflate raw invoices by $325,000, and supported revenue growth of 73.3% is materially below the claimed 300%. The investor needs corrected records and an explanation before relying on management’s forecasts.[^duplicates][^claims][^bank24][^bank25]
- **Collection risk remains:** $85,000 owed by Bright Foods was still open as of March 31, 2026. The $40,000 Riverside invoice was collected only in March 2026. Neither amount supports 2025 cash-basis revenue.[^open][^late]
- **Cash balances depend on funding:** year-end 2025 cash of $160,000 reconciles as $50,000 opening cash − $90,000 operating loss + $200,000 of loan/personal funding. A larger cash balance therefore does not demonstrate that operations are self-financing. The loan also creates a financing obligation whose repayment terms are absent from the inputs.[^bank24][^bank25][^funding]

### Conditions for reconsideration and team handoff

Reconsider only after management provides a corrected invoice register and cash-basis statements, explains the duplicate entries and unsupported claims, documents the Bright Foods collection outlook and funding obligations, and presents a credible plan to bring costs below revenue. As an illustration rather than a forecast, holding annual costs at $610,000 would require **$90,000 more net revenue**, or **17.3% above 2025 revenue**, to break even; holding revenue at $520,000 would require **$90,000 of cost reductions**, or **14.8% of 2025 costs**. Actual expansion could change costs, so neither calculation demonstrates that break-even is achievable.[^bank25]

The accountant and fraud auditor should validate the highlighted exceptions, while the Investment Manager should assess a forecast using corrected revenue and documented funding terms. **My current recommendation remains to pass; the conditions above are prerequisites for a fresh investment assessment, not approval of the current request.**
