# NovaCart Investment Decision Memo

**To:** Investment Committee  
**From:** Yasmine, Investment Manager  
**Date:** October 22, 2026  
**Request:** $500,000 for delivery coverage and customer acquisition

## Decision

**Pass on the current request as presented.** Reconciled 2025 net revenue was $520,000, but operating costs reached $610,000 and the loss widened to $90,000. The 12-month reference case leaves $21,083 at March 2027. The proposal gives no monthly use-of-funds plan, and customer activity and concentration need verification.

## Evidence

| Cash-basis result | 2024 | 2025 |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Revenue after refunds | $300,000 | $520,000 |
| Operating costs paid | $330,000 | $610,000 |
| Operating profit / (loss) | ($30,000) | ($90,000) |
| Operating margin | (10.0%) | (17.3%) |

Revenue grew 73.3% while costs grew 84.8%. Q1 and Q2 2025 were profitable; Q3 lost $1,387 and Q4 lost $126,295. The cash records do not support management’s claim of profitability since Q3. Its stated $300,000 to $1,200,000 growth arithmetic is 300%, but reconciled cash-basis revenue grew 73.3%.

The 2025 invoice rows total $1,000,000. The Accountant matched all 1,700 distinct paid receipt IDs and amounts to $896,000 of customer deposits across the export period, with no unmatched receipts or amount differences. Three repeated invoice copies account for $325,000; removing these, $85,000 of open Bright Foods invoices, and a $40,000 December invoice collected in March 2026 reconciles 2025 customer receipts to $550,000. After $30,000 of refunds, net revenue is $520,000. The $1.2 million claim also equals the $1.0 million raw invoice rows plus $200,000 of loan and personal-transfer funding. That coincidence warrants an explanation; the records do not establish intent.

Customer records show 1,100 distinct names invoiced in 2025, including five business accounts. The invoice export ends in December 2025 and cannot verify the 4,000 active-customer claim at the April 2026 pitch date. All 600 accounts seen in 2024 invoiced again in 2025, which measures annual invoicing retention, not current engagement or frequent ordering. Five business accounts represented 66.68% of adjusted 2025 invoiced value; the top three represented 48.15%. Household accounts grew 83.4% while household invoiced value fell 26.15%. These are billings, not cash collections.

## Cash outlook and conditions

Starting from $170,000 at March 31, 2026, the April 2026–March 2027 reference case ends at $21,083. Sensitivities end between $50,000 and $80,000. They assume no new funding or growth; the Q1 2026 pattern is a sparse-activity sensitivity. The $500,000 request is excluded because timing and use are unspecified.

Reconsider after management provides a corrected invoice register and cash-basis statements; a dated customer roster, active-customer definition, and evidence of major-account continuity; support for Bright Foods collection; documentation of financing; and a monthly use-of-funds plan with break-even milestones.
